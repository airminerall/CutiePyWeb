The idea

Python, made approachable.

CutiePy is a playful learning space for people taking their first steps into programming. We pair clear explanations with an interactive place to experiment, make mistakes, and try again.

Learn by doing

Every lesson gives you a concept, a small example, and a place to run it immediately.

Built as a team

CutiePy is created by students who want programming fundamentals to feel less intimidating and more human.
